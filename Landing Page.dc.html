The Gap

Community-serving organizations can often run into the following gaps

01

Decision-making gap

Community experience is often absent from organizational decision-making.

02

Navigation & influence gap

Communities lack clear pathways to navigate and influence organizations.

03

Engagement-to-outcomes gap

Engagement processes don’t consistently translate into meaningful outcomes.

04

Access & understanding gap

Policies and processes can be difficult for communities to access and understand.

This can lead to a weak connection between the organization and the community and creates long-term risks to organizational trust and legitimacy.

Flagship offer

Organizational–Community Alignment Review

A comprehensive assessment of how organizational policies, governance, services, and engagement practices align with community experience. The review identifies barriers, opportunities, and practical recommendations to strengthen decisionmaking, accessibility, accountability, and public trust.

Framework

I look at a problem through the following sequence

Experience→Organization→Gap→Intervention→Outcome

Experience

What is the community experience?

Organization

What organization systems, policies, processes, decisions, or assumptions affect the community experience?

Gap

Where is organizational intention differing from community reality?

Intervention

What needs to change?

Outcome

What does better look like for both the organization and the community?
