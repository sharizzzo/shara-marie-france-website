I see organizations from both sides.

I’m Shara Marie-France, a fractional organizational advisor.

Having lived in seven countries and attended fifteen schools, I have spent much of my life navigating unfamiliar systems, cultures, and organizations.

Seven years of working inside public institutions has given me an insider’s perspective on how organizations are designed and how they operate. And being a member of multiple underserved communities has given me an outside perspective on how they are experienced by the people they serve.

I’ve consistently seen that organizations are built by people who understand systems, but not always by people who navigate them. As a result, gaps emerge between organizational intentions and community realities.

I’ve spent most of my career inside institutions, translating community experience into practical ways to close those gaps.

And now I’m doing it independently, across organizations, as an outside advisor.

  • Institutional experience in government and public institutions across policy, governance, and labour relations
