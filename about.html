About Me

I see organizations from both sides.

I'm Shara Marie-France, an independent organizational advisor with a background in law, policy, governance, and community advocacy. I help organizations identify where their intentions and community experience diverge and develop practical ways to close that gap. My experience navigating institutions across seven countries, combined with working within public systems, gives me both an inside and outside perspective on how organizations operate and how communities experience them.
