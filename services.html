How I Work

I look beneath the surface to create lasting change.

Above the Surface

What's visible. Symptoms, events, outcomes, behaviors.

Below the Surface

What's underneath. Systems, policies, processes, structures, decisions, assumptions, incentives, norms, and culture.

I gather information, perspectives and context to understand the problem beyond its surface.

Before I offer an opinion, I listen. I talk with the people who run your programs and the people who use them, and I read what your organization has written down about how things are supposed to work. The distance between those two accounts is usually where the real story starts.

I connect patterns across systems, policies, structures, decisions, communications and behavior to determine the root causes.

Once I can see what's happening, I trace it back through your policies, processes and decisions to find where it starts. Most problems turn out to be built into how things work, not caused by any one person or team. Naming the root cause makes it much easier to fix without pointing fingers.

I translate the diagnosis into changes to systems, policies, governance, communications, processes or practices.

Then we get practical. I help you decide what to change first, and I put it in terms your team can act on with the time and budget you really have. The goal is change that holds after I've gone, built into how your organization runs day to day.

Flagship Offer

Initial Review

When the people you serve aren’t using what you offer, and you can’t figure out why.

Sign-ups are low. The drop-in sessions are half empty. Community trust is slipping. You’ve tried the obvious fixes and nothing’s working.

That’s the type of problem this review focuses on. I look at your policies, practices and engagement methods. Then I compare them to how communities actually experience them. Usually, the barrier is smaller and more fixable than it looks from the inside.

You leave with a clear diagnosis of why it’s happening, a practical plan for what to fix first, and a richer picture of the people you serve.

Community Insight & Organizational Assessment

When you sense something isn’t working but aren’t sure where.

Maybe it’s a feeling in staff meetings. Maybe it’s complaints that don’t add up to a clear pattern. This assessment finds the barriers, gaps, and struggles the people you serve are facing and why they are happening.

I review your policies, services, governance and communications in detail. Then I design and lead engagement with your community and stakeholders to hear about their experience from them directly.

You leave with clear priorities for improving decision-making, accessibility, accountability, and community trust.

Implementation Support

Good plans often stall in day-to-day operations.

The strategy’s approved, yet nothing is changing. I work alongside your team to build the new approach into policy, practice, and governance, so it doesn’t depend on one champion or one good quarter.

I create a practical implementation plan, facilitate collaboration with stakeholders and across departments, and help leadership and staff build their capacity to sustain the change.

You leave with lasting changes that strengthen accountability and trust between your organization and your community.
