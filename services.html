How I Work

I look beneath the surface to create lasting change.

Above the Surface

What's visible. Symptoms, events, outcomes, behaviors.

Below the Surface

What's underneath. Systems, policies, processes, structures, decisions, assumptions, incentives, norms, and culture.

01Understand

What is happening?

I gather information, perspectives and context to understand the problem beyond its surface.

02Diagnose

Why is it happening?

I connect patterns across systems, policies, structures, decisions, communications and behavior to determine the root causes.

03Change

What needs to be different?

I translate the diagnosis into changes to systems, policies, governance, communications, processes or practices.
