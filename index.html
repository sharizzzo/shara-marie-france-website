The Gap

Community-serving organizations can often run into the following gaps

01

Decision-making gap

Community experience is often absent from organizational decision-making.

02

Navigation & influence gap

Communities lack clear pathways to navigate and influence organizations.

03

Engagement-to-outcomes gap

Engagement processes don't consistently translate into meaningful outcomes.

04

Access & understanding gap

Policies and processes can be difficult for communities to access and understand.

This can lead to a weak connection between the organization and the community and creates long-term risks to organizational trust and legitimacy.

How I Work

I look beneath the surface to create lasting change.

Above the Surface

What's visible. Symptoms, events, outcomes, behaviors.

Below the Surface

What's underneath. Systems, policies, processes, structures, decisions, assumptions, incentives, norms, and culture.

01Understand

What is happening?

I gather information, perspectives and context to understand the problem beyond its surface.

02Diagnose

Why is it happening?

I connect patterns across systems, policies, structures, decisions, communications and behavior to determine the root causes.

03Change

What needs to be different?

I translate the diagnosis into changes to systems, policies, governance, communications, processes or practices.
