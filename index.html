You're a community-serving organization

Your intentions are good. You checked every box. So why isn't […]it working for people?

What makes sense for your organization doesn't always work for your community. The more familiar you are with your side of things, the harder it is to see the full picture of your community's experience. And that blind spot is an organizational risk that can get expensive.

Work With Me

It can start with lack of engagement. More complaints. People leaving without explanation. Staff absorb the frustration. Over time, it turns into lost funding, or public scrutiny like a news story or legal challenge.

It's easy to miss, and a survey won't catch it. Because, like everything else in the organization, it's designed from the inside out.

Community experience tells you how you'll do in the long term, but many organizations were taught to focus on this quarter, this budget, this reporting period.

What's measured is what's easy to count and report upward. And community feedback may get collected but is outweighed by the next deadline.

This is Where I Come In.
